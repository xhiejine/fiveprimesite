Privacy Policy.

Last updated: 9 November 2026. This policy explains how Five Prime Nigeria Limited handles information you share through this website.

Information we receive

This website does not store what you type. The quote form on the Contact page prepares a message that you choose to send by email or WhatsApp. That message may include your name, phone number, email address, the product or service you want, quantity, delivery location and any note you add. We receive it only when you send it.

How we use it

We use the details you send to reply to your enquiry, confirm availability, price and delivery, and carry out any order you place. We do not sell your information.

Third-party services

Sending an enquiry by WhatsApp or email passes your message through those services, which have their own privacy terms. This site also loads fonts from Google Fonts and an embedded Google Map on the Contact page, so Google may receive technical details such as your IP address when those load. We do not run advertising or analytics trackers on this site.

Keeping and sharing information

We keep enquiry messages only as long as needed to handle your request and for ordinary business records. We share information only where needed to fulfil your request or where the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete the details you have sent us by contacting us using the details below.
